# External data audit v2

Harvard Dataverse DOI 10.7910/DVN/YLWCSU version 2 reports CC0 1.0. Downloaded the 203,763-byte PDF, 37,701-byte segment table and a 4,194,304-byte prefix of the 100-taxi table. The full five taxi tables each contain roughly 327–331 MB. The PDF identifies measured API timestamps rounded to minutes, segment IDs, varying capacity and occupied counts. observedK/diffK fields describe simulated taxi coverage and measurement differences. The pilot excludes all these simulated columns.

The complete-line prefix contains 65,188 rows, six segments and dates June 13–July 24, 2013. Five segments have 12,096 rows each; the sixth is truncated at 4,708 rows. This is a file-order convenience sample. The segment table supplies WGS84 endpoints. Receipt time and explicit timezone are unavailable in the PDF. Instant availability and naive timestamp processing are declared assumptions. Upstream filtering removed implausible segments. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/YLWCSU)

The official Melbourne 2019 page links a 717.1 MB ZIP with 42.7 million events. A 2,097,152-byte ZIP prefix was downloaded and decompressed with a two-million-byte output ceiling. The resulting 11,535 complete rows cover 12 bays. Fields include DeviceId, BayId, ArrivalTime, DepartureTime, DurationMinutes and VehiclePresent. Times parse as month/day/year with AM/PM and no offset. Durations are integer minutes and can round short events to zero. The prefix is unsorted and sparsely samples bay histories. It cannot establish uninterrupted coverage. No Melbourne forecast scores were computed. [2019 source](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/on-street-car-parking-sensor-data-2019/)

The 2018 page advertises a 504 MB archive with 30.2 million rows. It was inspected as a fallback and not downloaded. Both Melbourne historical catalogs identify CC BY 4.0. Source-local clocks need Australia/Melbourne DST rules before continuous reconstruction. [2018 source](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/on-street-car-parking-sensor-data-2018/)

Official SFMTA hourly data remain a separate product. Unknown seconds can describe coverage but do not independently label wrong measurements. The guide documents time-weighted occupancy and outage exclusions. No official hourly CSV was downloaded or used. The previously reviewed guide and research/sfpark_external_validation.md specify required fields. Archive-specific reuse terms remain unresolved.

The Harvard adapter excluded 6,122 rows with nonpositive capacity or occupied counts outside physical bounds, retaining 59,066 rows before duplicate-key handling. Future-date test counts were 9,028 / 8,920 / 8,704 per condition at 30/60/120 minutes. Count and capacity exclusions can disproportionately remove restricted hours; the sample is conditional on valid monitored capacity.
