# Generalizability

The pilot includes future dates at observed sites and one held-out site per dataset, reserved by lexicographic ID. Training and calibration exclude that site. Model fitting starts independently in each city. This tests limited temporal and site transport, not transfer of a Birmingham-trained model to SFpark.

The Harvard byte prefix contains only six of the documented 420 segments, and the sixth has partial temporal coverage. Output counts expose eligible examples in each design. A representative full-segment sample and repeated held-site folds remain necessary. WGS84 geometry was acquired but is not used to claim spatial graph modeling or learned interactions. No Melbourne forecasting or SFMTA hourly evaluation has been executed.

Measured degraded held-site M2 MAE was 3.87/5.11/7.71 percentage points in Birmingham and 6.81/9.36/14.65 in Harvard SFpark. Corresponding fixed-gate M3 errors were 12.60/13.53/14.82 and 9.92/12.09/16.48. Held-site test counts were 111/86/73 in Birmingham and 1862/1850/1826 in SFpark. These small and single-site comparisons have limited representativeness. Quality-conditioned intervals deteriorated substantially on the held-out SFpark segment; see uncertainty_calibration.md.
