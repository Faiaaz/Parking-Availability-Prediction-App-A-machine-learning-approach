# Contribution assessment

The completed contribution is a reproducible controlled comparison of an explicit gate against a learner with identical quality features and a diagnostic of interval degradation. A quality indicator alone is conventional feature engineering. The fixed gate is a falsifiable design choice. Its mathematical form, simulations and residual intervals do not establish algorithmic novelty.

The measured comparison disfavors this gate. A defensible paper could report that negative result and distinguish detectable invalid values from plausible errors. A field-level novelty claim would require a structured review of quality-aware forecasting, missing-data baselines, expert fusion and dependent-time calibration, plus matched external replication. No priority claim has been established.
