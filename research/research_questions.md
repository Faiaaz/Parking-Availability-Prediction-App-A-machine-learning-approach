# Research questions and decision criteria

1. Can available indicators rank unreliable observations? Measure AUROC of 1−quality against injection assignment separately by failure type. Deterministic validity tests provide positive controls. Independent real-fault validation requires external quality labels.
2. Does enforcing a reliability gate reduce error beyond giving identical indicators to a conventional learner? Primary comparison is M3 minus M2 MAE on matched origins at each horizon. Evaluate clean and equally weighted degraded conditions separately. A positive difference disfavors the gate.
3. How do nominal 90% prediction intervals behave on measured clean and degraded inputs? Compare empirical coverage with 0.90 alongside interval width and interval score per horizon and severity.
4. Does an observed effect persist across later dates, unseen sites or a compatible dataset? The present later-week pilot tests temporal transport within known parks. Reserve a new period and entire sites for confirmatory comparisons. Cross-city evaluation requires a common target definition.
